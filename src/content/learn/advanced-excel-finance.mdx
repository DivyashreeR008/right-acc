Turn routine finance work into clear, repeatable spreadsheets. This course focuses on practical analysis and reporting—not shortcuts that are difficult to maintain.

## What you will learn

- Clean and validate imported transaction data.
- Use `SUMIFS`, `XLOOKUP` and date functions to reconcile records.
- Summarize revenue and expenses with PivotTables.
- Build a compact monthly reporting dashboard.
- Keep formulas auditable and protect key assumptions.

## A simple learning plan

1. **Prepare the data:** Standardize dates, account names and transaction references.
2. **Reconcile:** Use lookup and conditional-sum formulas to compare source records.
3. **Analyze:** Group activity by month, account and cost center with PivotTables.
4. **Present:** Add clear labels, a few useful charts and a notes section.

## Good spreadsheet habits

Keep raw data separate from calculations, use descriptive column names and avoid hard-coded assumptions inside formulas. Add a note describing the source and reporting period so another person can review your work.

## Practice safely

Use a copy of your monthly data. Remove personal or confidential information before sharing files with anyone.
