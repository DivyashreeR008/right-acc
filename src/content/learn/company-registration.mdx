The right business structure depends on ownership, funding plans and the work you are setting up. This course introduces two common Indian structures and the decisions to review before you register.

## Private limited company

A private limited company has shareholders and directors, with formal governance and recurring company-law filings. Founders often consider this structure when they expect to raise equity or offer employee stock options.

## Limited Liability Partnership

An LLP combines a partnership-style management model with limited liability. It may suit a professional partnership that does not plan to raise equity investment.

## Compare the decisions

- **Ownership:** Decide who will own and manage the business.
- **Funding:** Consider whether external equity investment is part of the plan.
- **Compliance:** Compare the filings, record keeping and professional costs for each structure.
- **Growth:** Consider future hiring, contracts and changes to ownership.

## Before you register

Prepare a list of owners, proposed activities and funding plans. Rules and filing requirements can change, so confirm the current position with a qualified company secretary or incorporation professional before making a decision.
