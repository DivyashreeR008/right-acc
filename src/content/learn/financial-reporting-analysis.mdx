Turn a set of monthly accounts into a useful management view. This course covers the structure of core reports, practical variance checks and how to communicate findings clearly.

## Start with reliable inputs

Confirm the reporting period, reconcile key balances and note any estimates or one-off transactions. Reports are only useful when their source data and assumptions are clear.

## Build a monthly reporting pack

- **Profit and loss:** Compare actual results with budget and the prior period.
- **Balance sheet:** Review cash, receivables, payables and material movements.
- **Cash flow:** Explain the timing of cash received and paid.
- **Commentary:** Highlight significant changes and the next questions to investigate.

## A practical review sequence

1. Check that the period is complete and balances reconcile.
2. Investigate material changes rather than reporting every fluctuation.
3. Separate recurring operating results from unusual items.
4. Write a short explanation using plain language and supporting figures.
5. Share the assumptions and limitations alongside the report.

## Keep it useful

Use a consistent format from month to month. A short report that answers a few important questions is more useful than a long pack without context.
