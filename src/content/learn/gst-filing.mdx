Goods and Services Tax (GST) applies to the supply of goods and services in India. A consistent filing routine helps a small business keep accurate records and meet its applicable due dates.

## What is GST?

GST is a destination-based, multi-stage tax. It replaced several earlier indirect taxes, including VAT, service tax and excise duty.

## GST Registration Threshold

Registration requirements depend on turnover, the type of supply and the state in which a business operates. Commonly cited thresholds include:

- **Goods suppliers**: ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for special category states)
- **Service providers**: ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs for special category states)

## Returns you may encounter

- **GSTR-1** records outward supplies and is filed monthly or quarterly, depending on eligibility.
- **GSTR-3B** summarizes tax liability and payment for the tax period.
- **GSTR-9** is an annual return for taxpayers who are required to file it.

## Benefits of GST Compliance

1. **Input tax credit**: Claim eligible credit on business purchases.
2. **Interstate trade**: Use a consistent tax process when supplying across state lines.
3. **Clear records**: Keep sales, purchases and tax payments easier to reconcile.
4. **Business readiness**: Some customers and tenders require a GST registration.

## Common Penalties

Late fees and penalties vary by return, circumstances and current rules. Check the latest GST portal guidance or consult a qualified professional before acting on a specific filing issue.
