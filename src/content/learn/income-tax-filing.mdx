Learn how to prepare for an individual income-tax return, organize supporting records and review the information before submission. This is a general learning guide; the right return and tax treatment depend on each person's circumstances and current rules.

## Gather your records

- Salary statements and Form 16, if applicable.
- Interest certificates and details of other income.
- Investment or deduction proofs relevant to your situation.
- Bank account details and the information needed to verify your return.

## Review before filing

1. Compare income details with the available tax information statements.
2. Check that deductions and tax credits are supported by records.
3. Reconcile tax already paid with the amounts shown in your documents.
4. Review the return summary before submitting it.
5. Complete the required verification step and keep the acknowledgement.

## Common points to check

Use the correct assessment year, confirm that bank details are current and check whether additional schedules apply to your income. If you have business income, capital gains or foreign assets, ask a qualified tax professional which return and disclosures apply.

## Keep a useful record

Store the filed return, acknowledgement and supporting documents securely. Never share passwords or one-time passcodes, and use a trusted channel when sharing tax documents.
